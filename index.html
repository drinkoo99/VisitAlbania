Food and Culture

A journey through Albania’s authentic flavors, stories, and traditions

Tavë Kosi

One of Albania’s most iconic dishes, combining tender lamb with a creamy yogurt and egg bake.

Flija

A traditional layered dish cooked slowly over open fire, symbolizing hospitality and family gatherings.

Fërgesë Tirane

A beloved Tirana specialty made with peppers, tomatoes, and creamy cottage cheese.

Albanian Oda

A traditional Albanian guest room where families welcomed visitors, celebrated events, and preserved cultural rituals.

Theth

The iconic church of Theth, nestled in the Albanian Alps, offers a serene glimpse into the region’s spiritual and cultural heritage.

Valle Tradicionale

Traditional Albanian folk dance, expressing community spirit, rhythm, and centuries-old cultural identity.

Events

Experience the traditions, festivals, and vibrant cultural moments that define Albania

24 DEC

Mangalem dresses in the colors of Onufri’s icons.

2025-12-24
Berat

Light projections of works by Onufri, Gustav Klimt, and Erblina Karali will illuminate Berat’s Mangalem neighborhood during “Mangalem’s Shades,” Dec 24–26 on Republika Boulevard, with DJs and a vibrant street festival in the city of a thousand windows.

24 DEC

Gzo n’Shkodër

2025-12-24
Shkodër

“Gzo n’Shkodër” lights up the city with year-end festivities: a magical setting, concerts by Shkodran singers on the promenade, and week-long celebrations from Dec 24–31 honoring the northern capital’s traditions.

31 DEC

Imagination in front of digital art in the first Gallery of Digital Arts in Albania

2025-12-31
Korçë

In Korça, visit GADK the first immersive Digital Arts Gallery in Albania to experience “Albanian Iconography” with projected masterpieces by Onufri and other icons. Open Tue–Sun, 12:00–20:00, the exhibit runs through Feb 29, 2024.
