Prepared 5 November 2026. **Everything below is now applied in this project**: all photos, the legal pages and the fact-check corrections. This file stays as the record of sources. Where it differs from the code, the code is current.

## 1. Photos

**All 29 places have a photo.** The list below still shows Korab-Koritnik under its old name. Each was opened and checked by eye. For 28, the author and licence come from the file's own Wikimedia Commons record, re-read through the Commons API. Mirror Beach comes from Unsplash.

- **Size:** the direct links are full-size originals. Resize them before use.
- **Share-alike:** CC BY-SA photos must stay under the same licence if you edit them, including crops and colour changes. Note your changes in the credit.
- **Rate limits:** Wikimedia may refuse automated downloads from these links. If so, open the file page and download it from there.
- **Panoramas:** Vjosa River, Lake Ohrid, Lake Prespa and Karaburun are very wide, so check them in tall crops such as 4:5 on mobile.
- **Korab-Koritnik:** see the fact check. Its correct name is Korab-Koritnik Nature Park.

### Fix these before publishing, or the text below will not be true

Your brief says the site collects nothing and stores only the language. The code does not fully match that:

| Finding | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The site also stores the chosen **region** in the browser for the session | `src/components/home/region-chooser.tsx` (`sessionStorage`, key `region`) | Drafts below already disclose it. Or remove it if you want language to be the only stored item. |
| Fonts load from **Google Fonts**, which sends each visitor's IP address to Google | `src/routes/__root.tsx`, line 101 | Ask Lovable to self-host the Epilogue font files. The drafts assume this is done. |
| The host will record server logs, including IP addresses | Hosting, not this code | Check your host's terms and fill in `[HOSTING PROVIDER]`. "Collects no personal data" alone would be inaccurate. |

| Contacting you by email gives you personal data | "Suggest a correction" link | Already covered in the drafts. |

## 3. Fact check

Lovable said you would paste the descriptions next. They are already in `src/data/destinations.ts`, so I checked those 42 places plus the events.

### Wrong or out of date: change these

| Place | Current claim | Problem | Suggested wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theth, Theth National Park | "inside Theth National Park" | Theth and Valbona were merged into the **Alps of Albania National Park**, established 26 January 2022. | "Theth is a village in a high valley of the Albanian Alps, inside the Alps of Albania National Park." Consider dropping the separate park page. |
| Homepage hero caption | "Kisha e Thethit, Theth National Park" | Same merger. | "Kisha e Thethit, Theth" |
| Valbona, Valbona Valley National Park | "inside the Valbona Valley National Park" | Same merger. | "Valbona is a valley in the far north, inside the Alps of Albania National Park, with a river running below limestone peaks." |
| Korab-Koritnik National Park (name and pages) | "National Park" | It is a **nature park** (Council of Ministers Decision No. 898, 21 Dec 2011; IUCN Category IV). AKT's page title says "National Park", but its text calls it a nature park. | Rename "Korab-Koritnik Nature Park". |
| Dhërmi | "the first village on the coast after the road comes down from Llogara Pass" | **Palasë** comes first, about 8 km north of Dhërmi. | "Dhërmi lies on the coast road south of Palasë, with an old village on the slope and beaches below." |
| Llogara Pass | "carries the road from Vlorë… down to the Ionian coast at Dhërmi" | The road reaches the coast at Palasë, and the **Llogara Tunnel** opened on 5 July 2024. | "The mountain road from Vlorë climbs over Llogara Pass and descends to the Ionian coast near Palasë.
